About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Cybersecurity at UNC Charlotte. My research focuses on IoT and cyber-physical systems security, AI security and privacy, and AI-assisted security analysis. I combine system analysis, formal methods, and experimental evaluation to uncover vulnerabilities and develop practical defenses.

I co-direct the SmartHome Lab, am a member of the Center for Energy Security and Reliability (CESAR), and serve as PI of the LLM Core Facility project. I received my Ph.D. from Temple University, advised by Xiaojiang Du, and my bachelor's degree in Information Security from USTC.

Research Interests

  • IoT & CPS security. Device semantics, timing attacks, anomaly detection, and cross-platform automation. Representative work: HAWatcher, Hidden Attributes, and IoT-Handler.
  • AI security & privacy. Language-model jailbreaks, backdoor defenses, and privacy in federated learning. Recent work: Activation Dilution, UDPU, and Does Efficiency Leak?
  • AI-assisted security analysis & verified automation. Connecting language-based reasoning with program analysis, formal verification, and physical simulation: PG-DSL, L2R, LLM-GridEval, and ICOAudit.
